Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

At FlexaBay.com, we prioritize the confidentiality, privacy, and security of our clients' academic work above all else. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle the documents, texts, and information you share with us during your session.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our plagiarism remediation and humanization services:

  • Documents & Drafts: Files (such as .docx, .pdf, .txt) and direct text paste uploads that you submit for similarity scanning and editing.
  • Order Requirements: Specifications including deadlines, writing level, citation styles, and custom instructions.
  • Contact Information: Your email address, used to share order tracking links, status updates, and final completed documents.

2. Data Handling & Absolute Confidentiality

To guarantee complete safety, FlexaBay adheres to a strict document management standard:

  • No Permanent Storage: Your uploaded documents are processed in an active encrypted session. They are immediately cleared from our processing servers once your service is completed or your tracking session expires.
  • No AI Model Training: We never use your essays, dissertations, research, or drafts to train machine learning models or AI algorithms. Your intellectual property remains entirely your own.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not disclose your files, personal info, or email address to academic institutions, detectors, or other external entities. Scanning results are completely private.

3. Security Protocols

We deploy premium security layers to protect your drafts from unauthorized access:

  • 256-Bit SSL Encryption: All file transfers, payment processing channels, and session communications are guarded by industry-grade Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption.
  • NDA-Bound Experts: Any manual review or professional academic polish is handled by PhD-qualified researchers who are under binding non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

4. Cookies & Usage Analytics

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preserve your cart state. We do not track your browsing activity across other websites or serve target advertising.

5. Contacting Us

If you have any questions about document security or data privacy protocols, please contact our support team at support@flexabay.com.
