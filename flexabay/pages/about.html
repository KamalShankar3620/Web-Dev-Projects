Our Background & Story

FlexaBay was founded in late 2024 by academic editors who grew increasingly frustrated by the rising popularity of hyper-aggressive AI checkers and similarity detectors. All too often, original work by diligent students was being falsely flagged, penalizing their hard work.

We realized that researchers needed more than a report telling them their papers were flagged. They needed an actionable, professional tool to fix the flags, re-phrase sentences naturally, and clean up references.

By blending smart semantic parsing with a network of NDA-bound professional editors, we created FlexaBay. Today, we're the leading platform for similarity remediation, helping students submit papers with complete security and peace of mind.

The Principles We Stand By

Absolute Security

Your papers are processed in encrypted sandboxes and scrubbed from our systems immediately after delivery. We do not store drafts or train AI models using your work.

Academic Integrity

We do not fabricate research. We polish flow, remove redundancy, bypass false detection signatures, and re-format referencing to keep your voice original.

Academic Standards

All professional editing and checks are overseen by doctoral academic scholars, ensuring discipline-specific tone, vocabulary accuracy, and formatting compliance.
