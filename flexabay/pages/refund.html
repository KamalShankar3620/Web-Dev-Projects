Refund Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

At FlexaBay.com, we are committed to providing top-tier academic editing and plagiarism remediation. We back our services with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. This Refund Policy details the terms and guidelines for refund requests.

1. Refund Eligibility Criteria

You may request a partial or full refund under the following circumstances:

  • Missed Deadlines: If we fail to deliver your completed document within the deadline specified in your order confirmation, you are eligible for a partial refund or discount matching the delay.
  • Similarity Target Failure: If the similarity score of the delivered document exceeds the target index ordered (e.g. Turnitin report shows similarity index over 15% when a under 10% index was ordered), and we fail to remediate it after revision.
  • Incorrect Order Delivery: If the completed files do not match the fundamental details of your brief (e.g., incorrect formatting style or missing requested dissertation chapters).

2. Verification Requirement

To prevent fraud and subjective claims, all refund requests relating to similarity scores or AI flags must be supported by official documentation:

  • Similarity Index Check: A PDF copy of the Turnitin, iThenticate, or equivalent similarity report showing the percentage match and flagged lines.
  • AI Flags: A PDF copy of the GPTZero or Turnitin AI checker report highlighting the segments flagged as AI-generated.

3. Revision-First Policy

We want to get your document right. In the event of quality discrepancies, we ask that you allow our expert writers a chance to revise the document. We will fast-track your corrections and deliver the revised file within 12 to 24 hours. If the revised document still fails to meet specifications, a refund will be processed.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are not granted in the following scenarios:

  • Grades & GPA Checks: FlexaBay provides academic editing, similarity remediation, and proofreading support. We do not issue refunds based on subjective grading or marks awarded by your professor or institution.
  • Self-Cancellation: If you request order cancellation after an assigned academic expert has actively begun working on your paper or completed the edit.
  • Incomplete Briefs: If quality deficiencies or delays occur because the client provided incorrect documents, missing prompts, or vague instructions during order submission.

5. Refund Processing Time

Once approved, refund credits are issued back to the original payment card or source account. Depending on your financial institution, card refunds take between 3 to 5 business days to reflect on your statement.
