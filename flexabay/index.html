Frequently asked questions

About FlexaBay's AI detection, plagiarism removal, and humanization tools

What is FlexaBay and how is it different?
FlexaBay is an AI-content and plagiarism remediation platform. Unlike basic checkers that only flag issues, FlexaBay runs a parallel scan and lets you fix flagged sections with targeted rewrites instead of starting from scratch.
How does the color-coded breakdown work?
After scanning, your document is highlighted in three colors: red for direct plagiarism (with source links), purple for AI-generated blocks, and orange for hybrid text — content that was AI-paraphrased from an existing source.
Which file formats do you support?
FlexaBay accepts .docx, .pdf, and .txt files up to 20 MB, as well as direct text paste. You can export cleaned documents back to .docx or .pdf once edits are applied.
What are the three humanization modes?
Light fixes phrasing and flow with minor vocabulary swaps. Balanced restructures sentence syntax to bypass major AI detectors — best for most cases. Deep performs a complete semantic rewrite, keeping the meaning while rebuilding every sentence from scratch.
How does the citation generator work?
For any red-flagged (plagiarism) section, FlexaBay auto-identifies the original source and generates a formatted citation in APA, MLA, or Chicago style. You can copy it or inject it directly into your document.
Can I rewrite only specific sections?
Yes — FlexaBay's targeted smart-removal lets you click any flagged block individually. You choose the rewrite intensity per section, so clean passages stay untouched while only problem areas are processed.
Which AI detectors does FlexaBay bypass?
The Balanced and Deep modes are optimized against GPTZero, Turnitin's AI module, Originality.ai, Copyleaks, and Winston AI. Results may vary as detector algorithms update.
Is my document kept private?
Documents are processed in an encrypted session and are never stored after your session ends. FlexaBay does not use your content to train models or share it with third parties.
How accurate is the AI detection scan?
FlexaBay combines a web-database plagiarism match with an independent LLM pattern scan, cross-referencing both results to reduce false positives. Accuracy benchmarks are published on our transparency page.
